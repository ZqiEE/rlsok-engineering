# Engineering roadmap

1. Qualify actual Gmsh meshes and CalculiX structural/solid-heat solutions against
   bounded reference cases. Report displacement, stress, temperature, heat flow,
   numerical error, failures, and scoped engineering files.
2. Complete English desktop asset localization and validate the new candidate
   under its own current runtime identity and authorization. A successful compile
   is not runtime acceptance or a standalone product release.
3. Provide a documented public native distribution and a reproducible path for an
   external AI to submit different real designs through the common entry point.
4. Qualify detailed flow with OpenFOAM, parameter uncertainty with OpenTURNS, and
   needed cross-tool exchange using Gmsh, preCICE and official adapters.
5. Broaden actual operating conditions, manufacturing tolerances, materials and
   physical prototype comparisons. Do not treat unmodeled phenomena as PASS.

Tool installation, an attractive 3D view, and fixed demonstration scripts are
not substitutes for engineering acceptance or an actual AI revision loop.

Resources for new professional runs remain independently bounded and authorized.
Valid existing evidence is reused; failed or exhausted stages are retained.
