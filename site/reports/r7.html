第 1 版 · ✗ 设计被平台拒绝 PORT_NOT_CONNECTED_IN_DUT: vout

AI 诊断（原文）：Initial design, so there are no native results yet. I did not simulate this netlist, and the expected values below are hand estimates. The 5 V ±3% band is ±150 mV. The R1/R2 tolerance alone moves the output by about ±0.1 V, so margin is tight and I centred the nominal output near 4.98 V. There is no efficiency or ripple requirement and no output capacitor in the testbench, so I chose a linear series regulator. It has no switching ripple and is insensitive to the ±5% R/L/C variation.

AI 修改（原文）：Series-pass linear regulator built from discrete parts. Q4 (TIP31-class NPN) is the pass emitter-follower and Q3 (BD139-class NPN) is its driver, which together form a Darlington. Q2 (2N3904-class NPN) is a common-emitter error amplifier. Its emitter sits on a 3.3 V zener reference (DZ1), biased from vin through RZ. Its base takes the output divider, R1 over R2a+R2b. Its collector load RC from vin drives the Darlington base. The loop forces mid-node = Vz + Vbe, about 3.99 V, so Vout is about 4.98 V (hand estimate), with loop gain of a few hundred. CC (47 nF) on the Q2 collector sets a dominant pole for stability. C1 (100 µF with 0.1 ohm ESR as RPAR_C1) on the output provides the filtering and load-step reservoir, since the testbench has no output cap. RPD gives the driver a minimum bias current, and the divider acts as a roughly 1 mA preload.

第 3 版 · ✓ 通过

AI 诊断（原文）：Round 2 passed all nominal checks (vout 4.909–5.018 V) but failed 1 of 40 tolerance cases. In mc_005, vout_min fell to 4.841 V against a 4.85 V limit. The cause is the resistive divider, which sets Vout = (Vz+Vbe)·(1+R1/(R2A+R2B)), a gain of about 1.24. Its ±5% R tolerances shift the ratio by about ±1.6%, which is about ±65 mV at the 4 V reference. In mc_005, R1 was -3.1% and R2A/R2B were +5%/+3.6%, which pulled Vout down by about 70 mV from a nominal minimum of 4.909 V. The nominal output also sat about 60 mV below 5.0 V, so there was little margin.

AI 修改（原文）：I removed the divider (R1, R2A, R2B). The error amplifier's base now connects straight to vout, so Vout = Vz + Vbe(Q2) and no R tolerance sets the output voltage. The zener model is now a ~4.3 V part (BV=4.24 with RS=10 and ~5 mA bias, so Vz is about 4.30 V). With Vbe of about 0.70 V this centres Vout near 5.0 V. The remaining R tolerances (RC, RZ) only change the Q2 bias current by a few percent, which moves Vbe by about 1 mV. Loop gain rises by about 1.24x because the divider attenuation is gone, which also tightens line and load regulation. Pass stage, compensation CC and output capacitor are unchanged. Hand estimate only, not simulated.
